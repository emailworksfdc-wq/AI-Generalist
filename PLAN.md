## Weekly rhythm

- **Mon–Fri:** live sessions or builds, ~2h/day

## When live sessions slip

If Outskill's actual schedule slips, keep your build-day dates fixed and slide the LIVE day forward — never skip a build day to "catch up" on lectures. Consumption expands to fill available time; production doesn't.
