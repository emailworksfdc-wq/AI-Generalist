# The 5 Signature Builds

The 30-day curriculum is organised around 5 shippable builds. Every video you watch and every day you spend should feed one of these. If a task can't be traced back to a build, cut it.

---

## 1. Personal Automation Hub — Week 1
n8n workflows that save ≥1 hour a day. Gmail triage + calendar prep + Slack digest.

Full spec: [builds/build-01-automation-hub/README.md](builds/build-01-automation-hub/README.md)

---

## 2. Media Studio One-Shot — Week 2
One prompt → 30-second branded video (script, image, video, voice-over) in ≤10 min.

Full spec: [builds/build-02-media-studio/README.md](builds/build-02-media-studio/README.md)

---

## 3. Voice Agent on a Phone Number — Week 3
Live phone number, real caller, real job (booking / FAQ / screening), transcripts logged.

Full spec: [builds/build-03-voice-agent/README.md](builds/build-03-voice-agent/README.md)

---

## 4. Jerry v1: AI Employee — Weeks 3–4
Cron-driven agent doing one job for you, autonomously, every day for a week.

Full spec: [builds/build-04-jerry/README.md](builds/build-04-jerry/README.md)

---

## 5. Capstone: Monetised Product — Week 4
One of the above wrapped with auth + pricing + Stripe + first paying customer.

Full spec: [builds/build-05-monetised-product/README.md](builds/build-05-monetised-product/README.md)

---

## The rule

Ship the current build before starting the next. If a week's checkpoint slips, cut scope on the *next* build, not on shipping the *current* one.
